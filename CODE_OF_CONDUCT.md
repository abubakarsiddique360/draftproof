# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want DraftProof to be a place where anyone can contribute without being harassed, dismissed or talked over. We pledge to make participation a respectful experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity or employer.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code, kind about people. Critique the change, not the contributor.
- Assume good faith, ask clarifying questions, and accept that reviewers may be wrong.
- Keep discussions on topic and technical.
- Respect that maintainers have limited time: small, focused pull requests get reviewed faster.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing private information (doxxing) without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or reviews.
- Dismissing reports of misconduct or retaliating against reporters.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any channel where you are representing the project.

## Reporting

Report incidents privately to the maintainer listed in `pyproject.toml`. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behaviour violates this code.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will apply the least severe response that resolves the situation, and will explain the decision to the people involved. Appeals can be sent to the same address within 14 days.
